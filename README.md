Substratism: Measuring Moral Bias Against AI

This research paper introduces and validates the concept of substratism, defined as a
moral bias that leads people to devalue artificial intelligence based solely on its non-biological makeup. 
Across five studies, the authors developed a concise eight-item scale to measure how individuals prioritize biological 
life over AI, even when the technology is described as having human-level intelligence or sentience. Their findings reveal that
while substratism overlaps with perceived threats from AI, it is psychologically distinct from traditional prejudices like racism or 
sexism. The studies demonstrate that higher levels of substratism predict a lower likelihood of supporting AI rights or choosing to save 
digital entities in moral dilemmas. Ultimately, the research provides a framework for quantifying how the physical substrate of an agent 
influences our willingness to grant it ethical consideration.
