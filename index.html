CLAIM BOUNDARY

Measurement is not ontology; sonification is not evidence.

The eight-item scale, published coefficients, sample sizes, and reliability range are research fixtures. Colours, geometry, synthesis, outcome reconstruction, and audiovisual coupling are authored explanatory receivers. The paper defines substratism descriptively and does not settle whether substrate differences are morally justified.
